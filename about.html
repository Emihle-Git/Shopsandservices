About This Directory

A free, local, human-curated guide to the shops and services of Uitenhage and Kariega.

Why we built this

Finding a reliable hardware store, locksmith, plumber or delivery service in Uitenhage (Kariega) usually means asking around on a community Facebook group or scrolling through outdated Google listings. This directory brings that information into one place: real addresses, real phone numbers, opening hours and photos for the shops and services people in our community actually use.

How listings are chosen

Every business on this site is based in or serves Uitenhage and Kariega, Eastern Cape. We verify contact details where possible and update listings as businesses change hours, move premises, or close down. If you spot anything out of date, please let us know — we rely on the community to help keep this accurate.

Who runs this site

This directory is an independently run local project, not affiliated with any of the businesses listed unless stated otherwise. It's funded by advertising so that it can stay free to browse and free for small local businesses to be listed.
