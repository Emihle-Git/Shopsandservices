# Before you go live — checklist

1. **Domain**: find-and-replace `YOURDOMAIN` with your real domain across every file
   (index.html, about.html, contact.html, add-listing.html, privacy-policy.html,
   terms-of-service.html, cookie-policy.html, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, ads.txt).
   Also replace every `info@YOURDOMAIN.co.za` email address.

2. **Google Analytics**: create a GA4 property at analytics.google.com, then replace
   `G-XXXXXXXXXX` in the `<head>` of every page with your real Measurement ID.

3. **Google AdSense**: after your site is approved (see chat for the full process),
   replace `ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX` in every page's `<head>`, uncomment the two
   `<ins class="adsbygoogle">` blocks in index.html, and add real `data-ad-slot` values
   from your AdSense dashboard.

4. **ads.txt**: replace `pub-0000000000000000` with your real AdSense publisher ID.

5. **"Cya" listing**: this entry in index.html has placeholder contact info
   (address "Uitenhage / Kariega, Eastern Cape" and a made-up phone number) —
   replace with the real business details or remove the listing before launch.
   Fake/placeholder NAP (name-address-phone) data hurts local SEO and can violate
   AdSense/Google Business content policies.

6. **Branding**: favicon.ico, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png, icons/icon-192.png,
   icons/icon-512.png, and images/og-cover.jpg are auto-generated placeholders
   (purple pin logo). Swap them for your real logo before launch if you have one.

7. **Google Search Console**: verify your domain, submit sitemap.xml.

8. **Google Business Profile**: create one and link it — huge for local search visibility.
