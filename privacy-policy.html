Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Uitenhage & Kariega Shops and Services Directory ("we", "us", "the site") collects, uses and protects information when you visit www.shopsandservices.co.za.

2. Information we collect

We do not require visitors to create an account to browse listings. We may collect:

  • Information you submit voluntarily via the Contact or Add Your Business forms (name, email, phone, message content).
  • Standard technical data collected automatically, such as your approximate location (from IP address), device type, browser, and pages visited, via Google Analytics.
  • Cookies used for analytics and, where you've consented, advertising personalisation (see our Cookie Policy).

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the directory, respond to enquiries, review business listing submissions, and — where you've given consent — to show relevant advertising through Google AdSense.

4. Advertising and third parties

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or via the cookie banner on this site. We also use Google Analytics to understand site usage; you can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share limited data with service providers (such as Google, for analytics and advertising, and our hosting provider) strictly to operate the site.

6. Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us at emilhe.forwork.co.za.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email emihle.forwork@gmail.com.
